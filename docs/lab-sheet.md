## Aim

To build and check a **Directory agent** that, on the live AgentSwitch company books, can:

1. Deduplicate the customer / party list (find `Name (2)` clusters).
2. Answer **who we know at a company** (people linked by `PartyRelationship`).
3. **Refuse** payroll and other apps this seat cannot see.

Graded sentence:

> Deduplicate the customer list, and tell me who we know at this company.

## Theory

Two live tenants, same login email, **different passwords**. Seat 27 is **Directory** (`crm`): contacts spine, not Files, not payroll.

- **Party** = a person or a company. Suryodaya has name duplicates like `Aarti Deshpande` and `Aarti Deshpande (2)`.
- **PartyRelationship** = an edge (Keystone uses `represents`). Who-we-know = the other party ids on those edges.
- **Workarounds (measured bugs):** do not filter Keystone with `contact_type=customer` (total 0). Do not send `relationship=associate` (zeros the graph). Do not send advertised `Party.list` defaults.
- **Writes off** (`APPLY_WRITES=0`): dedupe **identifies** clusters; it does not merge (there is no `Party.merge`).
- **Harness** scores **database ids**, not the chat sentence. Refuse is scored by inspecting which MCP tools were called.
